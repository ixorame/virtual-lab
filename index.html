Ohm’s Law

Theory

Ohm’s Law states that the current through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage across it.

V = IR

Procedure

  1. Connect battery, ammeter, resistor, and voltmeter.
  2. Set different voltage values.
  3. Note corresponding current values.
  4. Plot the V–I graph.
