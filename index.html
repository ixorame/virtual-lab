Theory

Ohm’s Law states that the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage across it, provided the temperature remains constant.

V = I × R

Procedure

  1. Set a suitable resistance value.
  2. Apply different voltage values.
  3. Calculate the corresponding current.
  4. Enter readings in the observation table.
  5. Plot the V–I graph.
