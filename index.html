Ohm’s Law

Theory

Ohm’s Law states that current is directly proportional to voltage.

V = IR

Procedure

  1. Connect battery, resistor, ammeter and voltmeter.
  2. Apply voltage.
  3. Measure current.
  4. Plot V–I graph.
