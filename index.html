Theory

Ohm’s Law states that the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage applied across it, provided the temperature and other physical conditions remain constant.

Mathematically,
V = I × R
where,
V = Voltage (Volts)
I = Current (Amperes)
R = Resistance (Ohms)

Procedure

  1. Select the Ohm’s Law experiment.
  2. Enter a suitable value of resistance.
  3. Apply different voltage values.
  4. Calculate the corresponding current.
  5. Enter the readings into the observation table.
  6. Plot the V–I graph using the readings.
