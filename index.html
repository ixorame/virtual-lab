Ohm’s Law

Theory

Ohm’s Law states that the current through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage across it.

V = IR

Procedure

  1. Connect the circuit components.
  2. Apply different voltages.
  3. Measure current values.
  4. Plot the V–I graph.
