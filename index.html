Ohm’s Law

Theory

Ohm’s Law states that the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage across it, provided temperature remains constant.

V = IR

Procedure

  1. Connect battery, resistor, ammeter, and voltmeter correctly.
  2. Set different voltage values.
  3. Note corresponding current values.
  4. Plot V–I graph.
