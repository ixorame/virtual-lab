Ohm's Law Experiment

Theory

Ohm’s Law states that current is directly proportional to voltage when temperature is constant.
V = I × R

Procedure

  1. Set resistance.
  2. Vary voltage.
  3. Note current.
  4. Add readings.
  5. Plot graph.
