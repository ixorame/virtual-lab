Ohm’s Law

Theory

Ohm’s Law states that the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage across it, provided temperature remains constant.

V = IR

Procedure

  1. Connect battery, resistor, ammeter, and voltmeter.
  2. Apply different voltage values.
  3. Measure corresponding current values.
  4. Plot the V–I graph.
